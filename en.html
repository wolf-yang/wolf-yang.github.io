01 / PROFILE

A little about me

I am a direct Ph.D. student at the Institute of Automation, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, jointly trained with Beijing Zhongguancun Academy. I received my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from Northwestern Polytechnical University. My research interests include LLM reasoning, agent architectures, and self-improvement, with a focus on past experience, component dependencies, and reliable long-horizon reasoning.
