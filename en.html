Research & exploration

Jul 2026 — Present

Agent self-improvement through experience graphs

Independent research exploration

Reviewing CoT, ReAct, multimodal reasoning, long-horizon reasoning reliability, and RSI-related work, with a focus on agent harness architectures and trajectory-driven component improvement.

Exploring an experience graph for coordinated improvement across components: linking successful and failed trajectories to harness components and dependencies to guide component selection and joint updates. Designing and validating the approach using open-source RSI frameworks.

Sep 2025 — Jun 2026

Dual-layer memory and knowledge retrieval for diagnostic agents

University–industry collaboration · AgentPool

Designed a cognitive architecture with dual-layer memory and hierarchical knowledge retrieval to address information loss during long reasoning, insufficient domain retrieval, and limited reuse of diagnostic experience.

Built structured working memory for key information, ruled-out hypotheses, and pending checks; organized diagnostic histories into long-term memory; developed hierarchical indexes over industrial manuals and experience, combining tree-based retrieval with cross-branch tag associations.

Jun 2024 — Sep 2024

Attention mechanisms for image denoising

Literature review & survey

Studied spatial attention, channel attention, and self-attention methods for image denoising.

Reviewed more than 50 papers and independently authored a survey.

May 2023 — May 2024

Ground target recognition with infrared–radar fusion

Multimodal feature fusion

Combined Transformer-based radar temporal features and ResNet-based infrared image features through hybrid attention for cross-modal fusion and target recognition.

Designed and implemented a fusion module incorporating spatial and channel attention, and contributed to platform development.
